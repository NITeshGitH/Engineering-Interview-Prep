# Engineering Interview Preparation Progress Tracker

> **Core Philosophy**: A topic is **NOT** complete merely because notes exist. Complete mastery requires three verified checkpoints:
> 1. 📝 **Notes Documented**: Clear, structured, original notes in your own words with citations.
> 2. 💻 **Hands-on Practice**: Solved coding problems, written test cases, designed schemas, or built pipelines.
> 3. 🎯 **Self-Test Passed**: Able to explain trade-offs and solve interview questions without referring to notes.

- ⚪ **Not Started** (0%)
- 🟡 **In Progress** (Notes in progress / initial study)
- 🔵 **Practice Done** (Notes complete + exercises solved)
- 🟢 **Interview Ready** (Notes + Practice + Mock/Self-Test passed)
